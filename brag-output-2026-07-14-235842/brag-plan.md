# Brag Plan: MsgFlash

## What is this app?
MsgFlash turns orders, payments, bookings, subscriptions and other business events into automatic WhatsApp communication.

## The angle
Business communication should not depend on someone remembering to send a message. The video treats MsgFlash as the operating layer between a business event and an informed customer: event in, flow runs, message queued, outcome visible.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Large editorial type: `Every business runs on events.` A compact event stream moves across the screen: order, payment, booking, shipment. The visual immediately anchors the product in real business activity, not generic automation.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The event stream resolves into a MsgFlash flow: `Business event → MsgFlash Flow → WhatsApp → Customer`.
- Three real landing visuals arrive sequentially: Business Flows, Campaigns, Analytics.
- The product promise lands as a readable statement: `The right message. Exactly when it matters.`

## Outro / punchline
MsgFlash mark and `Keep business flowing.` followed by `Start your free trial.`

## User flow worth showing
Landing-page product flow: business event → flow trigger → queued WhatsApp message → customer outcome. This is a marketing site, so the visual flow is the strongest truthful product moment available without a signed-in portal session.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a confident product launch card for a serious WhatsApp infrastructure product
- Interpretation: generous reading time, restrained motion, editorial typography, light surfaces, and only a few purposeful UI accents.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#FCFAF4`
- Accent: `#FFD600` with MsgFlash green `#25D366` for flow signals
- Text: `#171515`
- Display font: Geist-style sans fallback (`Arial`, heavy weight)
- Body font: Poppins-style sans fallback (`Arial`)
- Strongest visual element: light editorial sections, black type, yellow signal accents, and real product feature imagery.

## Share copy (draft)
MsgFlash turns the moments your business already creates into WhatsApp communication that happens at the right time. Business event in, customer informed.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm professional bed with restrained interface accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: fade in over the first 0.4s, low steady bed at roughly 0.18, fade under the final logo hold.
- Music cue guidance: bundled cue JSON copied to `composition/assets/music/`; use the strongest available cues near the flow reveal and final logo, while preserving text readability.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let the yellow signal line and image depth breathe slightly with RMS, never use a waveform or equalizer.
- SFX posture: sparse and polished; soft click/drop accents for event pills and feature visual arrivals.
- Audio-coupled moments: event pills appear in sequence, flow arrow resolves on the main reveal, final mark receives one quiet confirmation accent.
- Restraint rule: no loud impacts, no frenetic stutters, and no sound on every text line.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The business is moving — 3.5s
Warm MsgFlash background. The headline `Every business runs on events.` enters in two lines. Five compact pills appear in a measured sequence: Orders, Payments, Appointments, Subscriptions, Shipments.
Sequential/interaction: yes — five event pills arrive one by one, then hold together.
Audio intent: establish calm momentum with soft UI ticks.
Audio-coupled idea: card/drop accent on the first and last pill only.
Music: warm business bed, low volume.
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Connect the event to the customer — 4.5s
The event pills slide into a horizontal flow diagram: `Business event` → `MsgFlash Flow` → `WhatsApp` → `Customer`. The central MsgFlash step gets a yellow signal highlight. Supporting line: `The right message. Exactly when it matters.`
Sequential/interaction: yes — nodes resolve left to right.
Audio intent: one confident reveal without sounding like an alarm.
Audio-coupled idea: soft confirmation accent on the MsgFlash node.
Music: continue, slight lift at the flow reveal.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The product surface — 6.5s
Three real MsgFlash feature images appear as a horizontal editorial strip, one at a time: Business Flows, Campaigns, Analytics. Each image is paired with its product label, not a generic feature claim.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three image panels arrive with readable labels and settle into a single composition.
Audio intent: let the product feel tangible and complete.
Audio-coupled idea: light drop accent on the first panel; no repeated hits for every panel.
Music: steady and understated.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The promise — 3.5s
Large statement: `Orders. Payments. Bookings. Shipments.` then a smaller line: `Automated WhatsApp communication for every important business event.`
Sequential/interaction: yes — four words appear as a compact sequence and then lock into one line.
Audio intent: confident synthesis.
Audio-coupled idea: no per-word SFX; preserve copy clarity.
Music: begin gentle fade.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 2s
MsgFlash mark, `Keep business flowing.` and `Start your free trial.` on the light brand background. Hold long enough for the CTA to read.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: quiet, resolved close.
Audio-coupled idea: one subtle confirmation accent at the mark landing.
Music: fade out under the final hold.
Transition mood: clean hold

**Music mood for this video:** polished business / warm / restrained
**Audio summary:** A low, clean business bed supports the story while a handful of soft interface accents mark only the event sequence, flow resolution, and logo landing.
